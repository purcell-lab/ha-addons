Simulated bidirectional DC charger and EV (ISO 15118-20 DC_BPT) for isolated testing. Not a physical charger. Direction and power come only from the test central system.

Simulated EV

Plug in waits for authorisation. Start the transaction from the test central system (RequestStartTransaction). To discharge, send an OCPP 2.1 TxProfile period with operationMode CentralSetpoint and a negative setpoint in W.

Discovery self-test

ISO 15118 discovery (SDP) from the EV end of the private link (v2g1) to the charger (v2g0): unicast, as CI's probe sends it, and multicast to ff02::1, as the simulated EV sends it. Runs once at start; a packet capture on both link ends shows where a request stops.
